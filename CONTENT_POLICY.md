# Content Policy

Tide is a general-purpose downloader and media player.

Tide does **not** provide content search, torrent indexing, pirate-resource
discovery, content catalogs, recommendations, or DRM/paywall bypass.
Tide does not upload, relay, or host user content.

Tide only processes sources the **user** explicitly provides. Tide does
not judge whether a user has the rights to a given source.

**Users are responsible for ensuring that they have the necessary rights
to obtain, copy, store, play, or distribute the content they use with
Tide.** Copyright and related laws differ between jurisdictions; comply
with the laws that apply to you.

## 内容政策（中文）

Tide 是一个通用的下载器与媒体播放工具。

Tide **不**提供内容搜索、Torrent 索引、盗版资源发现、内容目录、推荐，
也**不**绕过 DRM 或付费墙；不中转、不上传、不托管用户内容。

Tide 只处理**用户主动提供**的来源，不判断用户是否对特定内容拥有权利。

**用户应自行确保对通过 Tide 获取、复制、存储、播放或分发的内容拥有
合法权利。** 各司法辖区的著作权与网络传播法规不同，请遵守适用的法律。

该政策不构成任何法律承诺；"下载工具本身中立"不等于"任何使用方式都合法"。
